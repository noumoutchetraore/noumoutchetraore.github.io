About

I am an economist working on development in West Africa, with a particular focus on Côte d'Ivoire.

I recently completed a research master's degree in economics at Anadolu Üniversitesi, Türkiye, with a thesis on the transition to a knowledge-based economy in Côte d'Ivoire. Before that, I studied development economics at Université Félix Houphouët-Boigny in Abidjan.

My current questions concern the place of informality in West African economies, the small share of manufacturing, and the way firms and workers respond to formalisation and industrial policy. I am interested in how public policy can work with the real behaviour of economic agents rather than against it.

Research interests: development economics, informality, structural transformation, industrial policy, knowledge economy.

Research

Master's thesis

Transition to a Knowledge-Based Economy in Côte d'Ivoire: Panel Data Analysis

Anadolu Üniversitesi, 2026. Supervisor: Assoc. Prof. Dr. Burak Tuğberk Tosunoğlu

This thesis examines the relationship between the knowledge economy and economic growth in ECOWAS countries, and its policy implications for Côte d'Ivoire. It combines a Basic Scorecard analysis based on the four pillars of the World Bank's Knowledge Assessment Methodology with short-run panel estimations over 2010–2022. The results suggest that growth in the region is not yet supported by an integrated structure of knowledge economy pillars, and point to the importance of education, institutional capacity and a more integrated innovation system.

Working paper, in progress

Trade Openness, Productive Power and Industrialisation: A Threshold Analysis Inspired by Friedrich List

2026

Building on Friedrich List's concept of productive forces, this paper constructs a composite Index of Productive Power using principal component analysis, and applies a Hansen (1999) panel threshold regression to manufacturing value added in developing countries over 2000–2022.
